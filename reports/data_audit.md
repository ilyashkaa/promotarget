### Repeat Purchase Analysis

After removing confirmed cross-sheet duplicates and applying the preliminary valid product purchase rules, the dataset contains 788,499 transaction lines associated with 5,862 customers.

Of these customers, 4,181 (71.32%) made purchases on at least two distinct calendar days during the observation period.

The median observed interval between consecutive purchase days is 32 days, and 49.07% of observed intervals are no longer than 30 days.

A 30-day prediction horizon is retained as the initial business assumption. Its suitability will be evaluated using time-based customer snapshots and the resulting target distribution.

These statistics describe observed historical purchasing behavior and should not be interpreted as the positive class rate or the causal effect of marketing campaigns.
